## Private source records - do not place in `public/`

The uploaded originals remain outside the website:

- `Discovery.pdf` - working discovery outline
- `staples_scan_1674138443882.pdf` - mixed police, EMT, medical examiner, witness, news, and court records
- `staples_scan_1674138522685.pdf` - mixed court filings, correspondence, emails, and agency records
- `Weems, Gary 1993-3665.pdf` - compiled sensitive record set with duplicates, identification material, medical material, and death-scene content

Before any original page is published, create a redacted derivative and record the source filename and page number. Do not publish autopsy/death-scene images, IDs, addresses, signatures, birth dates, private emails, phone numbers, or other protected information.
