# Justice Denied — Easy Content Guide

Update public information in `app/page.tsx`.

1. **Donation:** Search for `Add your Venmo` and replace the placeholder button with a link to Venmo, PayPal, GoFundMe, or Stripe.
2. **Festivals:** Edit the three entries inside `const festivals` at the top.
3. **Archive:** Edit `const files`. Put public PDFs in `public/archive/`, then change placeholder buttons to links.
4. **Speaker photo:** Add `public/jestina-speaker.jpg` and replace the JWR placeholder.
5. **Videos:** Add YouTube embeds whenever a new video is ready.
6. **Email:** Search for `JesTTMI@gmail.com` if the contact changes.

Before publishing records, remove private addresses, phone numbers, signatures, birth dates, medical information, and private identifiers.
